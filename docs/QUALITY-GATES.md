# Review criteria

Vision review: GOAL/CAP IDs unique; confirmed statements cite PO evidence; scope, goals and audience coherent; success criteria not invented; impact on downstream stories documented. Story review: As a/I want/so that meaningful, scoped rules trace to decisions, every active FR/NFR mapped to an AC and TC, tests executable in principle, Mermaid matches text, and human approval recorded if claiming Ready (team agreement). Neither local readiness nor Product Vision is a mandatory Scrum artifact.

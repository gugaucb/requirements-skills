# Traceability model

`GOAL → CAP → EPIC (optional) → US → FR/NFR → AC → TC`. `DG` may support a US/FR/AC and `DEC` may justify any node. Higher-level relations live in `PRODUCT-VISION.md` and `BACKLOG.md`; lower-level authoritative matrix lives in each `US-XXX.md`. Avoid duplicate sources of truth. A capability can link directly to stories without an epic. Draft gaps must be explicit; readiness requires all active requirements linked to verifiable AC and TC. For revisions, retain IDs and record affected downstream items. Simple matching of tokens cannot verify business semantic coverage.

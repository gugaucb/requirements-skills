# Order Status Portal

Lets customers follow their own orders.

## Language

**Order**:
A customer's request to receive goods. _Status: Confirmed (Ana, 2026-05-02)_
_Avoid_: Purchase, transaction

**Order status**:
The stage an order has reached: Placed, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled or On hold. _Status: Confirmed (DEC-001, DEC-003)_
_Avoid_: State, phase

**Owner**:
The customer who placed the order. _Status: Proposed_
_Avoid_: Buyer, account holder
